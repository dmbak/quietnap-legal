# Privacy Policy — QuietNap

Last updated: June 2026

QuietNap respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information We Collect

QuietNap does not collect or store personal data on external servers.

QuietNap does not use accounts, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking services.

## Local App Data

QuietNap stores app data locally on your device. This may include:

* selected sounds and alarms;
* nap, sleep and reset durations;
* saved sound mixes;
* mixer settings such as layer volume and tone;
* completed session history;
* Rhythm summaries and duration suggestions based on completed sessions;
* Premium, subscription or lifetime entitlement status;
* app preferences and settings.

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to QuietNap servers.

## Rhythm

QuietNap’s Rhythm view uses your completed sessions to show gentle local summaries, such as your usual time of day, frequently used sounds, completed durations and suggested nap duration.

These summaries are generated on your device. QuietNap does not upload or share this information.

## Health Data

If you enable Apple Health sync, QuietNap may write completed nap sessions to Apple Health as sleep records.

Health data is handled through Apple Health and only with your permission.

QuietNap does not upload or share health data.

If QuietNap reads Apple Health data in a future version, the app will request your permission and this policy will be updated to describe that use.

You can disable Apple Health sync at any time in the app settings or in the Apple Health permissions settings.

## Purchases and Subscriptions

QuietNap uses Apple’s in-app purchase system for Premium subscriptions and Lifetime purchases.

All payments, subscription renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple. QuietNap does not access your payment card information.

QuietNap may locally store whether Premium access is active, expired, restored or unlocked for life.

## Third-Party Services

QuietNap does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services.
